# Ventrilo.ai

> Ventrilo.ai is a San Francisco AI startup (founded 2023; backed by Andreessen Horowitz, Bloomberg Beta, Conviction, Rain Capital, 112 Capital, and Gil Capital) building an AI browser assistant delivered as a Chrome extension. It connects your open tabs, remembers context, auto-fills forms, and drafts and rewrites text in place. Ventrilo.ai publishes no public developer API, SDKs, or API documentation; its api.ventrilo.ai backend is private. Not related to the classic Ventrilo VoIP software.
